Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At CherryChic ("we", "us", or "our"), your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle data when you visit and use cherrychic.in.

1. Client-Side Processing (Zero Data Storage)

Our tools (including the Safe Zone Checker, Carousel Splitter, and Color Extractor) are built with a privacy-first architecture. All image, video, and text processing happens 100% locally in your device's web browser. We do not upload, transmit, or store your photos, videos, drafts, or captions on our servers.

2. Cookies and Google AdSense

To keep CherryChic free, we use third-party advertising companies like Google AdSense to serve ads when you visit our website.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.

3. LocalStorage

Some tools (like our Caption Spacer) use your browser's LocalStorage to save your text drafts so you don't lose them if you accidentally refresh the page. This data remains exclusively on your personal device and cannot be accessed by us.

4. Analytics

We may use basic website analytics (such as Google Analytics) to understand aggregated traffic patterns. This data is strictly statistical and does not identify individual users.
