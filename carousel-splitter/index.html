How to Create Seamless Instagram Carousels

Creating a seamless Instagram carousel is one of the most effective ways to tell a story, showcase a panoramic landscape, or share a detailed tutorial on social media. By splitting a single ultra-wide image into multiple square or portrait tiles, users can swipe through your post smoothly, mimicking a continuous canvas. Not only does this encourage users to spend more time on your post (which boosts your algorithm ranking), but it also provides a highly aesthetic visual experience.

With our free Instagram Carousel Splitter, you don't need expensive software like Photoshop. Our tool uses HTML5 Canvas to precisely slice your images without any quality loss, directly in your browser. Since everything happens locally on your device, your images are never uploaded to a server, ensuring 100% privacy and lightning-fast processing speeds.

Recommended Image Dimensions

To get the best results, you need to prepare your base image with the correct aspect ratio. Instagram supports two primary formats for carousels: Square (1:1) and Portrait (4:5). Portrait is highly recommended because it takes up more vertical screen space, giving your content more visibility on the timeline.

  • Square (1:1): Each slide should be 1080x1080 pixels. If you want a 3-slide carousel, your starting panoramic image should be 3240x1080 pixels.
  • Portrait (4:5): Each slide should be 1080x1350 pixels. For a 3-slide carousel, your panoramic image needs to be exactly 3240x1350 pixels.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool reduce the image quality?

No, our splitter maintains the original resolution of your image. If your image perfectly matches the calculated dimensions, it will be sliced exactly pixel-for-pixel. We export the resulting slides in high-quality PNG format to prevent any compression artifacts.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

Absolutely not. The cropping and processing happen entirely within your web browser using JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas technologies. Your photos never leave your device.

Why are there gaps when I post to Instagram?

If you notice tiny gaps while previewing your post on the Instagram draft screen, don't worry! This is a known UI bug in the Instagram app preview. Once you actually publish the post, the slides will align perfectly seamless without any gaps.
