How Much Should Influencers Charge in 2026?

Setting your rate for brand sponsorships can be stressful. The industry standard has shifted away from simply pricing based on follower count. Brands now heavily prioritize average video views, engagement rate, and niche authority.

Formula for Calculating Engagement Rate and CPM

The standard baseline calculation many agencies use in 2026 relies on a blended CPM (Cost Per Mille / thousand views) plus a flat production fee:

  • Base Production Fee: Approximately $100 per 10,000 followers. This covers your time conceptualizing, filming, and editing.
  • Viewership CPM: $20 to $30 per 1,000 average views. Video views are the true indicator of reach. Brands don't care if you have 1 million followers if your videos only average 5,000 views.
  • Rights Multipliers: Organic posting is the baseline. If a brand requests Paid Whitelisting (the ability to run your post as an ad), you must charge a premium of +20% to +50% for 30 days of usage.

Why Niche Dictates Your Pitch Strategy

As illustrated in the cheat sheet above, all audiences are not created equal in the eyes of advertisers. A creator reviewing enterprise SaaS software or personal finance tools has an audience primed for high-ticket conversions. A software company is willing to pay a $50 CPM because a single customer might be worth $2,000 in Lifetime Value (LTV).

Conversely, a prank or comedy channel might have 10x the views but a fraction of the purchasing intent. When using our Rate Calculator, always select your primary niche to ensure your baseline and aggressive pitch recommendations are properly weighted.

Handling Exclusivity and Whitelisting Demands

The most common mistake new creators make is granting unlimited usage rights in perpetuity. Never sign a contract that allows the brand "in perpetuity" usage for a flat fee. Always cap usage rights at 30, 60, or 90 days. If a brand wants to continue running your face in their Facebook ads after 30 days, they must pay a renewal fee (typically 10-15% of the original contract value per month).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Paid Whitelisting / Ad Boosting?

Whitelisting gives a brand permission to run paid ads through your handle. Since this extends the reach and lifecycle of the post, you should always charge an additional 20-30% on top of your base rate for 30 days of usage.

Should I charge more for specific niches?

Yes. Highly targeted niches like Finance, Tech, and B2B software command higher CPMs because the audience is more likely to make high-value purchases. Broad niches like Comedy or general Lifestyle typically command slightly lower CPMs.

What if a brand asks for exclusivity?

If a brand prevents you from working with their competitors for a set duration (e.g., 3 months), you are losing potential income. You should add a 30% to 50% exclusivity fee on top of your final rate.
